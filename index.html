Fortress Free-For-All

A little stadium oval with walls, ramps and banked turns. Walk up to a car or a motorcycle in the infield parking lot and press E to drive. In a vehicle, Tab opens its tuning: acceleration, brakes, steering, weight, size, suspension. Invite friends to race and crash into each other.

Every weapon lies on the ground behind you with its ammo: walk over one to pick it up (3 at most, plus the knife; Q drops one). Dummies stand down the lanes at 5, 10, 20, 30 and 45 m, and a blast pit to the left. A dummy has 40 health, shown as a circle over its head from blue (full) to red (1 left): it turns green when you've done lethal damage, then resets. Tab opens the weapon settings.

You're joining a friend's game. The host picks the level.

Pick a name, then join.

Needs a mouse and keyboard. Best in landscape.
