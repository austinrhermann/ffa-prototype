Free for all

A little stadium oval with walls, ramps and banked turns. Walk up to a car or a motorcycle in the infield parking lot and press E to drive. In a vehicle, Tab opens its tuning: acceleration, brakes, steering, weight, size, suspension. Invite friends to race and crash into each other.

Defend your fort from greedy pirates who want to crack you like an egg and scrap your home for parts.

You're joining a friend's game. The host picks the level. In the fort, if the tower has room you're the Master or the Apprentice; otherwise you join the pirates.

Pick a name, then join.

Alone you are always the Master. The second player takes the other role; anyone after that attacks.
